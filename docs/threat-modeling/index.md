# Threat Modeling

This section covers threat modeling frameworks and risk management practices. Threat modeling is the structured process of identifying security threats, assessing their risk, and prioritising mitigations before vulnerabilities are introduced.

## Core Topics

- **[Frameworks](frameworks.md)** - STRIDE, PASTA, MITRE ATT&CK, attack trees, DREAD, and guidance on choosing a framework for your context
- **[Risk Management](risk-management.md)** - Risk assessment fundamentals, likelihood and impact scoring, risk treatment options, blast radius analysis, risk communication, and risk registers

## Key Learning Objectives

After studying this section, you should understand:

- The purpose and timing of threat modeling in the development lifecycle
- STRIDE categories and how to apply them to system components
- PASTA's process-oriented approach to threat analysis
- How MITRE ATT&CK maps to threat modeling and detection
- Attack tree construction and analysis
- Risk scoring methodologies and prioritisation techniques
- Risk treatment options (accept, mitigate, transfer, avoid) and when to use each
- How to communicate risk to technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Blast radius estimation and containment strategies

!!! info "Prerequisites"
    Familiarity with basic security concepts and software architecture is assumed. For foundational reading, refer to [Threat Modeling: Designing for Security](https://shostack.org/books/threat-modeling-book) by Adam Shostack and the [OWASP Threat Modeling Guide](https://owasp.org/www-community/Threat_Modeling).
