# Cryptography & Identity

This section covers the cryptographic foundations and identity management principles essential for infrastructure and cloud security.

## Cryptographic Foundations

- **[Cryptographic Fundamentals](fundamentals.md)** - Encryption vs encoding vs hashing vs signing, symmetric and asymmetric encryption, block and stream ciphers, hashing functions, MACs, digital signatures, key exchange, and entropy

## Identity & Access

- **[Identity Management](identity.md)** - Identity lifecycle, access control models (RBAC, ABAC, ReBAC), service accounts, privileged access management (PAM), federated identity, and governance

## Authentication

Authentication protocols and mechanisms have their own dedicated section for deeper coverage.

- **[Explore Authentication Section](../authentication/index.md)** - Sessions, JWT, OAuth 2.0/OIDC, SAML, mTLS, WebAuthn, and Directory Services

## Key Learning Objectives

After studying this section, you should understand:

- The differences between encryption, encoding, hashing, and signing
- When to use symmetric vs asymmetric encryption
- How key exchange protocols establish secure channels
- Common cryptographic attacks and how to prevent them
- Identity lifecycle management and deprovisioning risks
- Access control models and their trade-offs
- Service account security in cloud environments
- Federated identity and its trust implications

!!! info "Prerequisites"
    No advanced mathematics background is required. For deeper cryptographic theory, refer to [Crypto 101](https://www.crypto101.io/) and [Practical Cryptography for Developers](https://cryptobook.nakov.com/).
